## Failures found by the tests and what was done
0. UI integration found six backend gaps (unlocked reads racing atomic saves on Windows, a cancel lost when the worker re-saved a stale job, retry leaving parts looking finished, review overwritten by a running worker, history without thumbnails, `~$` names sanitised before the check): fixed at the source (cancel flag file, locked reads/writes, retry resets parts to queued, history carries thumbnail + SOLIDWORKS info) with 4 tests.
1. Template path relative -> all 18 parts failed with "could not create a drawing from the template" (SOLIDWORKS resolves relative paths against its own cwd). Fixed: absolute paths everywhere; the failure had been reported per part, not as a crash.
2. Circular parts: no straight silhouette edges -> every overall dimension failed. Fixed: diameter dimension on the outer circle, thickness from an edge-on view, extreme-edge span check.
3. 18-hole flange: 18 separate callouts with crossing leaders. Fixed: bolt-circle recognition (one `NX` callout, one pitch-circle dimension across an opposite pair, equal-spacing note only when verified).
4. Half-circle arc edges dimensioned as a radius (38.9 vs 77.8 mm). Fixed: arcs get a diameter dimension, closed circles get the hole callout.
5. Text clipped outside the sheet / overlapping text (30 findings). Fixed: deterministic layout-repair pass (clamp into the usable area, push colliding text apart, view labels are fixed obstacles).
6. Hole callout selected a neighbouring step's circle (15.5 in vs 17 in). Fixed: match the hole's own radius.
7. Title block lines misplaced by sketch inference (unequal rows). Fixed: build with `SketchManager.AddToDB = True`.
8. Template document left open after `SaveAs3` renamed it. Fixed: re-read the title before closing; sweep of stale job documents at job start.
9. Part open in SOLIDWORKS with the same title as the working copy -> open failed. Fixed: unique working-copy title; warning when the open part has unsaved edits.
